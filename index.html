The 1930
Broadcaster

Reachy Mini hosts a wireless from a world that ended in 1930

✦✦

Powered by Alec Radford's Talkie-1930 — a 13 billion-parameter language model trained on 260 billion tokens of pre-1931 English text — and Kokoro-82M's posh British voice, this app turns your Reachy Mini into a 1920s BBC wireless announcer. It doesn't merely sound 1930. It thinks 1930.

▶  Install on Reachy Mini ⎇  View Source

How the Wireless Works

Ⅰ
🎙

You Speak

Reachy's four-microphone array captures your voice. faster-whisper transcribes it locally on the robot's Raspberry Pi — no cloud, no delay.

❧
Ⅱ
📡

Talkie Thinks

Your words travel to Talkie-1930, a language model whose entire world ended on 31 December 1930. It replies in the vocabulary, syntax, and worldview of the era.

❧
Ⅲ
📻

Reachy Broadcasts

Kokoro-82M speaks the reply in a measured Received Pronunciation baritone at 92% speed — the exact cadence of a 1930s BBC newsreader — while the robot's head and antennae move in sync.

Sample Broadcast

"What is electricity?"

"Electricity, my dear listener, is the invisible motive force that has transformed civilisation within living memory. It flows through copper wire as water through a conduit, powering the electric lamp, the wireless set, and the tram. One need not grasp its mathematical mysteries to appreciate that we live in an age of electrical wonder."

— Talkie-1930, spoken by Reachy Mini in the voice of bm_george

Installation

Via the App Store

Find The 1930 Broadcaster in the Reachy Mini app store and press Install.

Open App Store

Via the CLI

uv pip install reachy-mini
# on Raspberry Pi:
sudo apt-get install espeak-ng
reachy-mini-app-assistant install \
  reachy-mini-talkie
reachy-mini-app-assistant start \
  reachy-mini-talkie

Requirements

  • Reachy Mini Wireless (Raspberry Pi 5, 8 GB RAM)
  • Network connection for Talkie-1930 inference
  • espeak-ng (apt) for Kokoro phonemisation
  • Python ≥ 3.10
Language Model Talkie-1930 · Alec Radford, Nick Levine & David Duvenaud
Text-to-Speech Kokoro-82M · hexgrad
Robot SDK Reachy Mini SDK · Pollen Robotics
Speech Recognition faster-whisper · SYSTRAN
